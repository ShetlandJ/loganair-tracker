What's going on

Loganair doesn't price each seat separately. Every flight has a fixed ladder of fares. On Sumburgh–Edinburgh right now the steps are …. Each step has a number of seats allocated to it, and a step can close for two reasons:

  • It sells out: busy days (Fridays, Sundays, Christmas) lose the cheap steps early.
  • The calendar says so: cheap steps close at fixed points before departure whether or not the plane is full. That's how a flight a third full can have only the top fare left.

This is standard airline "revenue management": late bookers tend to be people who have to travel and will pay anyway. Collecting data over the coming weeks will show exactly when each step closes.

Caveats
  • This is the cheapest fare per day, not per flight. An evening flight can cost more than the day's lowest fare.
  • Prices are per person, one way, excluding ADS / Island Resident discounts.
  • Seat counts aren't captured yet.
