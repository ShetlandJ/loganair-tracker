How's the Loganair fare cut going?

A local software engineer has been tracking Loganair's fares since October, checking every few hours. So when the cut was announced, we could see exactly what changed.

The good news is real. The most expensive fares to Edinburgh and Glasgow have come down by more than a fifth, and booking last minute is now cheaper.

But at the same time, the cheapest fare went up on all six Sumburgh routes. The £149.99 fare to Edinburgh, on sale on 153 days before the change, has gone. And on Aberdeen, most flights got a little dearer.

Loganair says if you plan ahead you'll still get its lowest fares. That's true, but those lowest fares are now higher than they were. Islanders deserve the full picture, and I've asked Loganair to explain.

Keeping it watertight

Loganair will check every word of this, so the graphics only say what the data supports. If the wording changes, keep it on the left-hand side.

Safe to say

  • The cheapest fare rose on all six Sumburgh routes, by £3 to £22, when the top fares were cut.
  • The highest fare to Edinburgh or Glasgow fell from about £386 to £300, as Loganair said.
  • £149.99 Sumburgh to Edinburgh was on 153 dates. It's on none now.
  • On Aberdeen, most flights in the following 60 days got slightly dearer.
  • The new fares were on sale from 4 November, 13 days before the announcement.

Avoid

  • "The cut isn't real" or "Loganair misled people."The top fares fell as stated.
  • "Fares went up overall."957 dates rose and 720 fell, but the average change was a £1 fall.
  • "The cut was paid for by raising the cheapest fares."That needs seat sales, which only Loganair has.
  • "£149.99 was the normal price."Tracking began on 10 Oct, and it may have been a promotional fare.
  • Anything about residents' ADS fares or Fly Flex tickets.The data can't show those reliably.

Figures are the cheapest one-way fare Loganair's website calendar showed for each day, not the price of a particular flight. The full briefing and data are public at github.com/shetlandj/loganair-tracker.
